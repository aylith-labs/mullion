Adds Mullion to Terminal, between MTPuTTY and Putty.

Mullion is my fork of Windows Terminal (MIT, free), under active development. What it adds:

- **Hover previews**: a GitHub issue or PR, a Jira key, a Slack permalink or a local file (Markdown rendered, highlighted source, images, PDFs) previews in a card, without leaving the terminal.
- **Tabs on any edge**: top, bottom, or a vertical sidebar on the left or right.
- **Sessions come back**: reopening the terminal reattaches the tmux or zellij session a pane was running, instead of opening a fresh shell.
- **WSL paths are links**, resolved in the right distribution.
